Call flow

  1. Exotel places the call.
  2. When the person answers, Exotel opens the secure audio stream.
  3. The AI connects to Gemini Live and gives the company greeting.
  4. The AI listens and answers factual questions from the active Knowledge Base.
  5. The call is recorded and the system saves status, transcript, duration and summary.
